Our tours

Five ways to see Sri Lanka

Every tour is a starting point. Change the length, swap a stop or mix two tours together, and tell us how you want to get around and where you want to sleep.

South coast

Relaxation on the Waves

Slow days beside the Indian Ocean. Snorkel the reef at Hikkaduwa, take a surf lesson at Weligama, walk the ramparts of Galle Fort and watch for whales off Mirissa.

  • Suggested4 days
  • Best monthsNov to Apr
  • Good forCouples, families, first-time surfers

Highlights

  • Snorkelling and glass-bottom boat rides over the Hikkaduwa coral
  • Turtle hatchery visit along the coast road
  • Beginner surf lessons at Weligama and Mirissa
  • Sunset at Galle Fort and on Unawatuna beach
  • Whale and dolphin watching from Mirissa in season
  • Coconut Tree Hill at Mirissa for the evening light

Sample route

  1. Day 1Negombo to Hikkaduwa, then a free beach afternoon
  2. Day 2Reef snorkelling, then Galle Fort and Unawatuna
  3. Day 3Surf lesson at Weligama, sunset at Mirissa
  4. Day 4Morning whale watching, then the drive back

Whale watching depends on the season, so we plan around it.

Hill country

Scenic Routes, Waterfalls and Hikes

Cool air, green valleys and long views. Ride through tea country, hike to viewpoints around Ella and stand beside some of the island's tallest waterfalls.

  • Suggested5 days
  • Best monthsJan to Apr
  • Good forHikers, photographers, active families

Highlights

  • Nine Arch Bridge near Demodara, with the trains passing
  • Little Adam's Peak, an easy walk with wide views
  • Ravana Falls and Diyaluma Falls
  • Horton Plains and World's End at first light
  • Tea estate and factory visit around Nuwara Eliya
  • Kandy lake and the Temple of the Tooth

Sample route

  1. Day 1Negombo to Kandy, lake walk and the Temple of the Tooth
  2. Day 2Kandy to Nuwara Eliya through the tea country
  3. Day 3Early Horton Plains and World's End, then on to Ella
  4. Day 4Little Adam's Peak, Nine Arch Bridge, Ravana Falls
  5. Day 5Diyaluma Falls, then the drive back

Waterfalls run fullest after rain, so we adjust the order to the weather.

Cultural Triangle

Cultural Wonders and Ancient Cities

Walk through more than 2,000 years of history. Climb the rock fortress at Sigiriya, explore the sacred city of Anuradhapura and the ruins of Polonnaruwa, and visit the cave temples at Dambulla.

  • Suggested4 to 5 days
  • Best monthsFeb to Sep
  • Good forHistory lovers, families, photographers

Highlights

  • Sigiriya Rock Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Pidurangala Rock for the view back across to Sigiriya
  • Dambulla Cave Temple and its painted ceilings
  • Sri Maha Bodhi and Ruwanwelisaya in Anuradhapura
  • Polonnaruwa ruins, easy to explore by bicycle
  • Temple of the Tooth in Kandy

Sample route

  1. Day 1Negombo to Anuradhapura, sacred city at sunset
  2. Day 2Mihintale, then the cave temples at Dambulla
  3. Day 3Pidurangala at sunrise, then Sigiriya Rock
  4. Day 4Polonnaruwa ancient city
  5. Day 5Kandy and the Temple of the Tooth

Visit a temple in the right clothes? We tell you what to wear before each stop.

Wildlife

One with the Wild

Dawn jeep safaris in the national parks, where leopards, elephants, sloth bears and hundreds of bird species live. Yala has one of the highest leopard densities in the world.

  • Suggested3 to 4 days
  • Best monthsFeb to Jul
  • Good forFamilies, photographers, nature lovers

Highlights

  • Early morning jeep safari in Yala National Park
  • Elephant herds at Udawalawe
  • Elephant Transit Home at feeding time
  • Birdwatching at the Bundala wetlands
  • Wild peafowl, crocodiles and water buffalo along the tracks
  • Optional whale watching at Mirissa in season

Sample route

  1. Day 1Negombo to Udawalawe, afternoon safari and the Transit Home
  2. Day 2Dawn safari in Yala, rest through the heat of the day
  3. Day 3Second Yala drive, or birdwatching at Bundala
  4. Day 4Optional Mirissa whales, then the drive back

Park rules and closures change through the year, so we confirm the plan before you travel.

Before you book

What does a tour cost?

The price depends on the route, the number of guests, the vehicle and whether accommodation is included. Send a request with your dates and group size and we will reply with a quote.

Can children join?

Yes. Every tour is family friendly, and we set the pace and the stops around the youngest traveller.

Can I use my own hotel?

Yes. You can choose your own accommodation, or we can include it in the package.

How do we travel between places?

By private vehicle. The size of the vehicle depends on how many guests are travelling and where you are going.

Can I change a package?

Yes. Every package is a starting point. It can be shortened, extended or mixed with another one.

When is the best time to travel?

Sri Lanka has two monsoons, so some part of the island is always in season. The south and west coasts are best from about November to April, the hill country from January to April and wildlife safaris from February to July. Tell us your dates and we will suggest the best route.
